WELCOME TO RETROCLOCK

A red-digit bedside clock, like the one from 1985, for your phone.

GETTING STARTED

  • Install it: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then runs full screen, even offline.
  • Turn it sideways for the big digits.
  • Tap the clock to dim it for the night.
  • Tap ALARM to set a wake-up time, pick a sound, and choose a digit size.
  • Tap NOISE for steady sleep sound. Tune it with the sliders and save your favorite.

ONE IMPORTANT THING

RetroClock is a nightstand clock. The alarm only rings while the app is open on screen, so leave it running, plugged in, on your nightstand. It plays even with the silent switch on. Each time you open it, tap TAP TO ARM SOUND once.
